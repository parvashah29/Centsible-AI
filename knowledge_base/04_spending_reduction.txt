Budgeting and Reducing Spending — Practical Tactics

GROCERY AND FOOD SPENDING
Groceries and dining out are typically the largest variable expenses for most households. Here is how to reduce them intelligently.

Grocery reduction tactics:
1. Plan meals for the week before shopping — reduces impulse buys by 30–40%.
2. Always shop with a list. Never shop hungry.
3. Compare unit prices (per kg or per litre) not package prices.
4. Buy staples (rice, dal, oil, spices) in bulk from local stores — much cheaper than quick commerce apps.
5. Use quick-commerce apps (Zepto, Blinkit, BigBasket) for convenience only, not as your primary grocery channel — you pay a 15–25% premium for speed.
6. Reduce packaged and processed foods — they are expensive per calorie.
7. Utilise cashback on grocery credit cards (HDFC MoneyBack, Axis Flipkart card give 5% on BigBasket).

Dining/Food delivery reduction tactics:
1. Set a firm monthly dining budget — say ₹3,000 — and stop when it is used.
2. Cook in batch on Sundays for 3–4 weekdays.
3. Treat food delivery (Zomato, Swiggy) as a luxury, not a default.
4. Use office canteen/mess instead of ordering lunch — can save ₹3,000–₹6,000/month.
5. Carry a water bottle and home-made snacks to avoid expensive cafe purchases.

TRANSPORT SPENDING REDUCTION
1. If you commute daily by cab, calculate the monthly cost. Compare it to a metro pass + auto for last mile.
2. Use Uber/Ola pool or share rides where possible.
3. For outstation travel, book trains via IRCTC 60–90 days in advance for 50–70% savings over last-minute fares.
4. FASTag auto-recharge — prevents missed discounts at toll plazas.
5. If you drive, maintain tyre pressure (improves fuel efficiency by up to 3%).

SUBSCRIPTION AUDIT (ENTERTAINMENT SAVINGS)
Most people are subscribed to services they forgot about. Do a monthly audit:
1. List all active subscriptions: Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hotstar, Spotify, Audible, YouTube Premium, LinkedIn, Adobe, etc.
2. Cancel anything you have not used in 60+ days.
3. Share plan costs with family (Netflix family plan, Amazon prime sharing).
4. Rotate subscriptions — subscribe to one service at a time, cancel when finished, move to next.
5. Use free alternatives: YouTube (free with ads), Spotify free tier, public library apps for books.

SHOPPING AND IMPULSE BUYING
1. Apply the 24-hour rule: Wait 24 hours before any unplanned purchase above ₹500.
2. Apply the 10x rule: Would you work 10 hours to afford this item? If not, skip it.
3. Unsubscribe from brand marketing emails — they are engineered to trigger impulse purchases.
4. Delete saved card details from shopping apps — the friction of re-entering details reduces impulse buys significantly.
5. Set a monthly "fun money" budget — once it is gone, no more non-essential shopping until next month.

UTILITY BILL REDUCTION
1. Switch to LED bulbs if you haven't — saves 80% on lighting electricity.
2. Unplug chargers and appliances when not in use (standby power accounts for 5–10% of electricity bills).
3. Use a smart power strip for your home theatre/computer setup.
4. Negotiate your internet plan annually — providers offer better deals to customers who call and threaten to leave.
5. Install a low-flow showerhead to reduce water bills.
